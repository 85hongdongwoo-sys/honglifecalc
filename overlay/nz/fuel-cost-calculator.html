Your numbers

Quick answer (2026): fuel cost = km × L/100 km ÷ 100 × price a litre. Auckland to Wellington (640 km) at 7 L/100 km and $2.60 a litre (example price) costs about $116.48 one way, $232.96 return. Diesel cars and EVs also pay road user charges: the standard light-vehicle rate is $76 per 1,000 km.

How the calculator works

Example first. You drive 640 km. Your car uses 7 litres every 100 km. You need 640 × 7 ÷ 100 = 44.8 litres. At $2.60 a litre you pay 44.8 × 2.60 = $116.48.

  • Fuel consumption (L/100 km): how many litres your car uses to drive 100 km. Lower is better.
  • Litres needed = km × L/100 km ÷ 100.
  • Fuel cost = litres × price per litre.
  • Road user charges (RUC): a charge per 1,000 km for diesel vehicles and EVs. The calculator adds km × rate ÷ 1,000.

Tips to spend less

  • Compare stations. Prices can differ a lot between towns and brands. Discount cards and apps can cut a few cents a litre.
  • Use your real fuel use. Hills, towing and open-road speed raise fuel use.
  • Check tyre pressure. Soft tyres and a roof box both increase fuel use.
  • Diesel or EV? Remember RUC: at the standard $76 per 1,000 km it adds 7.6 cents a km.

Fuel cost FAQ

How do I work out the fuel cost of a trip?

Litres = km × L/100 km ÷ 100. Cost = litres × price per litre. Example: 640 km at 7 L/100 km uses 44.8 litres. At $2.60 a litre that is $116.48.

How much does it cost to drive from Auckland to Wellington?

About $116 in fuel one way for a car using 7 L/100 km, at $2.60 a litre (640 km). A return trip is about $233. Split between 2 people, that is $116.48 each.

How do I convert km per litre to L/100 km?

Divide 100 by your km per litre. 20 km/L = 5 L/100 km. 14 km/L = 7.1 L/100 km. 10 km/L = 10 L/100 km.

Do I need to add road user charges?

Yes, if you drive a diesel car or an EV, because they pay road user charges. The standard light-vehicle rate (RUC type 1) is $76 per 1,000 km, so a 640 km trip adds $48.64. NZTA also lists a temporary reduction, so check the rate that applies to you. For a petrol car leave the box at 0.

How much do I spend on fuel commuting?

30 km a day for 20 days is 600 km. At 8 L/100 km that is 48 litres. At $2.60 a litre you pay $124.80 a month.

What fuel price should I use?

Use the price on the pump where you fill up. Prices differ by region, brand and station, and change often, so an average may not match what you pay.
