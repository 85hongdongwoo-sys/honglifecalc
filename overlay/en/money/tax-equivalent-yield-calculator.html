What a tax-free yield is worth in 2027

Tax-equivalent yield = tax-free yield ÷ (1 − your tax rate). With the 2027 federal brackets, a 3.5% municipal bond is worth 4.61% taxable in the 24% bracket and 6.13% in the 37% bracket once the 3.8% net investment income tax is added.

  1. Find your federal bracket for 2027 in the table below (taxable income, after deductions).
  2. Add 3.8% if your modified AGI is above $200,000 (single), $250,000 (joint) or $125,000 (separate) — the NIIT applies to taxable interest above those amounts.
  3. Add your state rate only if the muni is from your own state (out-of-state munis are usually state-taxable).
  4. Divide the muni yield by (1 − total rate) and compare with the taxable bond’s yield.

Understanding the tax-equivalent yield calculator — concept and principle

Interest from most US municipal bonds is free of federal income tax, so a lower municipal yield can be worth more than a higher taxable yield. The tax-equivalent yield tells you what a taxable bond would have to pay to leave you with the same income after tax.

How it is calculated

  • Combined tax rate on taxable interest = federal rate + net investment income tax + state rate (state tax simply added, ignoring any deduction).
  • Tax-equivalent yield = tax-free yield ÷ (1 − combined rate).
  • If the municipal bond is from another state, your state usually taxes its interest, so its yield is first reduced by the state rate.
  • After-tax yield of a taxable bond = taxable yield × (1 − combined rate).

The principle

What matters to an investor is the income kept after tax. A taxable bond loses part of its interest to tax; a tax-free bond does not. Dividing the tax-free yield by the share of taxable interest you keep, (1 − t), gives the pre-tax yield that would leave you with the same amount. The higher your tax bracket, the more a tax exemption is worth, which is why municipal bonds appeal most to high earners.

Worked example

Default inputs: municipal yield 4%, federal rate 32%, net investment income tax 3.8%, no state tax, bond free of state tax, taxable alternative 5.5%.

  1. Combined tax rate = 32 + 3.8 + 0 = 35.8%.
  2. Tax-equivalent yield = 4 ÷ (1 − 0.358) ≈ 6.23%.
  3. After-tax yield of the tax-free bond = 4%.
  4. After-tax yield of the 5.5% taxable bond = 5.5 × 0.642 ≈ 3.53%.
  5. Tax-free bond is better after tax, by 0.47 points.

How to read the result

If the taxable bond yields less than the tax-equivalent yield, the municipal bond leaves you with more after tax. Compare bonds of similar credit quality and maturity; a higher yield may reflect higher risk. Investors in low tax brackets may find taxable bonds better.

Common mistakes

  • Using your average tax rate instead of your marginal rate.
  • Forgetting state tax on out-of-state municipal bonds.
  • Ignoring the alternative minimum tax on some private-activity municipal bonds.
  • Comparing bonds with different credit ratings or call features.
Keep in mind: Tax treatment depends on your situation and current law. This is not tax or investment advice; confirm with a tax professional.

Frequently asked questions

What is the tax-equivalent yield formula?

Tax-equivalent yield = tax-free yield ÷ (1 − tax rate). For example, 3.5% ÷ (1 − 0.24) = 4.61%.

Which tax rate should I use for 2027?

Your 2027 federal marginal bracket (10% to 37%; the 24% bracket covers $109,126–$208,325 for single filers), plus 3.8% NIIT if your modified AGI is above $200,000 single or $250,000 joint, plus your state rate for in-state bonds.

Is the NIIT threshold adjusted for inflation?

The IRS lists fixed thresholds of $200,000 (single or head of household), $250,000 (married filing jointly) and $125,000 (married filing separately).

What is the tax-equivalent yield formula?

Tax-free yield ÷ (1 − tax rate).

Are municipal bonds always tax-free?

Interest on most is exempt from federal income tax, but state tax and the AMT may apply in some cases.

Which tax rate should I use?

Your marginal rate, the rate on your next dollar of income, including any extra tax on investment income.
