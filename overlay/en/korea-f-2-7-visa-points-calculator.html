How the F-2-7 points system works

Step 1 – be in an eligible group. Points only matter if you fit one of five groups:

  • Professionals: 3 years in a row in Korea on E-1 to E-7 or D-5 to D-9 status (not E-6-2 or E-7-2 to E-7-4), meeting the extension rules.
  • Listed-company staff: working (or hired) at a KOSPI/KOSDAQ company as a manager or professional.
  • High-tech industry workers: in a government-listed advanced-technology field, earning at least 1.5 × the previous year’s GNI per capita.
  • Korean graduates: a Korean master’s or PhD and a professional job within 5 years of graduating.
  • Potential talent: a STEM master’s or PhD from one of the specialised universities or institutes listed in the notice.

Step 2 – add up your points. The four basic items can give 130 points. Bonuses are capped at 40, so the highest possible total is 170. You need 80.

Step 3 – check you are not disqualified. Some records rule you out completely, whatever your score: a criminal fine of ₩3 million or more in the last 3 years, a prison-level sentence in the last 5 years, 3 or more immigration violations in 3 years totalling ₩5 million or more, or false documents.

Tips to raise your score – and common mistakes

  • Korean is usually the quickest win. Going from TOPIK 2 to TOPIK 4 adds 10 points; TOPIK 5 adds 15.
  • Watch the income lines. ₩29.9 million scores 10, but ₩30 million scores 30 – a 20-point jump. ₩39.9 million → ₩40 million is another +10.
  • KIIP counts twice: stage 5 gives 20 in the Korean item and completing it adds a 10-point bonus.
  • Age moves your score. Turning 30 costs 2 points and turning 40 costs 8, so it can pay to apply before a birthday.
  • Mistake: using gross salary from a contract. Immigration looks at taxable income on the official income certificate.
  • Mistake: counting a completion certificate or volunteer work of under 1 year – neither gets points.

F-2-7 visa FAQ

How many points do you need for the F-2-7 visa?

At least 80 points out of a possible 170. The basic items (age, education, Korean and income) give up to 130, bonus items up to 40, and deductions can take away up to 70.

How do I get 80 points for F-2-7?

A typical passing mix is age 25–29 (25), a master’s in engineering (20), TOPIK 4 (15) and ₩40–50 million income (40) = 100 points. Without much income or Korean it is hard: a 33-year-old business graduate with TOPIK 2 and ₩34 million scores 73.

How many points is TOPIK worth for F-2-7?

TOPIK 1 = 3, TOPIK 2 = 5, TOPIK 3 = 10, TOPIK 4 = 15 and TOPIK 5 or 6 = 20 points. KIIP stages 1–5 score the same. Completing KIIP stage 5 also gives a separate +10 bonus.

How much income do I need for F-2-7?

There is no single minimum in the points table, but income gives up to 60 points: ₩30 million earns 30, ₩40 million 40, ₩50 million 45 and ₩100 million or more 60. Income below the yearly minimum wage scores 0. Only the high-tech industry group needs at least 1.5 × GNI per capita (about ₩78.9 million on the Bank of Korea’s 2025 figure).

Can I apply for F-2-7 on an E-7 visa?

Yes, if you have lived in Korea legally for 3 years in a row on E-1 to E-7 or D-5 to D-9 status and meet the extension requirements. E-6-2 and E-7-2 to E-7-4 holders are excluded from this group.

Does a criminal record or immigration fine stop me getting F-2-7?

Small records cost points: an immigration fine of ₩1–3 million is −20 and a criminal fine under ₩2 million is −20. A criminal fine of ₩3 million or more in the last 3 years, a prison-level sentence in the last 5 years, or 3+ immigration violations totalling ₩5 million in 3 years makes you ineligible.

When did the current F-2-7 points table start?

The Ministry of Justice notice No. 2025-408 took effect on 24 October 2025. Applications filed before that date were judged under the old notice. It was still the current version when we checked on 23 November 2026.
