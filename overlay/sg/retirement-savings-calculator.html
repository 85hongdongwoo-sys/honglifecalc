Your numbers

Quick answer (2026): divide your yearly retirement spending by your withdrawal rate. With the 4% rule, $36,000 a year needs $900,000; if CPF LIFE pays you $1,500 a month, you only need $450,000. CPF LIFE payouts can start from 65.

How it works

Example first. You want to spend $52,000 a year. At a 4% withdrawal rate you need $52,000 ÷ 0.04 = $1,300,000. You have $65,000 now and save $1,300 a month at 6% a year. The calculator adds interest every month and finds you reach the target in about 26 years.

  • Withdrawal rate = the share of your savings you spend in the first year.
  • Target = yearly spending you need from savings ÷ withdrawal rate.
  • CPF LIFE = a monthly payout for life from the CPF Board. It lowers the spending your own savings must cover.
  • Compound growth = you earn returns on your returns. The calculator adds them monthly.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Count CPF LIFE. Leaving it out can make your target look far bigger than it is.
  • Use today's dollars. The calculator doesn't add inflation, so enter spending at today's prices and a return after inflation.
  • Plan for healthcare. Medical costs usually rise with age.
  • Test a lower return. Try 4% to see how much later you would reach the target.
  • Housing matters. A paid-off home lowers how much you need each year.

Retirement savings FAQ

How much do I need to retire in Singapore?

Start from what you will spend in a year and divide by your withdrawal rate. With the 4% rule, $36,000 a year needs $900,000 of savings. If CPF LIFE pays you $1,500 a month ($18,000 a year), you only need to cover $18,000 from savings: $450,000.

What is the 4% rule?

It is a rule of thumb. You take out 4% of your savings in the first year of retirement, then roughly the same amount each year after, adjusted for prices. Savings of $1,300,000 give $52,000 in the first year. It came from US market data, so many people use 3–3.5% to be safer.

Does CPF LIFE reduce how much I need to save?

Yes. CPF LIFE pays you a monthly income for life, and payouts can start from 65. Every $1,000 a month it pays covers $12,000 a year, which at 4% is $300,000 less you need to save yourself. Use your own estimate from the CPF Board.

What are the CPF retirement sums for 2026?

For members turning 55 in 2026: Basic Retirement Sum $110,200 and Full Retirement Sum $220,400. The Enhanced Retirement Sum is $440,800 in 2026, whenever you turn 55. They set how much is kept in your Retirement Account for CPF LIFE payouts.

How long will it take to reach my target?

With the default numbers – $65,000 saved, $1,300 a month and 6% a year – it takes about 26 years to reach $1,300,000. Saving more each month or starting earlier shortens this a lot.

Is a 6% return realistic?

Nobody can promise it. Returns go up and down, and inflation lowers what your money can buy. Try 4% or 5% as well to see a more careful result.
