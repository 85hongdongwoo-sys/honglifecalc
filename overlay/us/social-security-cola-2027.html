Social Security COLA 2027: 3.6%

Your January 2027 check, the new limits, and when the money arrives.

Social Security and SSI benefits rise 3.6% in 2027, the largest increase in five years. The average retired worker goes from about $2,095 to $2,170 a month (+$75). The higher Social Security checks start in January 2027; SSI recipients get theirs on December 31, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Social Security COLA for 2027?

3.6%, announced by the Social Security Administration on October 14, 2026.

How much will the average Social Security check be in 2027?

About $2,170 a month for all retired workers, up from $2,095 (SSA estimate for January 2027).

When does the 2027 COLA show up?

In the January 2027 Social Security payment. SSI recipients receive the increase on December 31, 2026.

What is the 2027 SSI payment?

$1,029 a month for an individual and $1,544 for a couple (federal amounts).

What is the Social Security wage base for 2027?

$192,300. Earnings above this are not subject to the 6.2% Social Security tax.

How does Social Security round the COLA?

The new benefit is rounded down to the next lower dollar, which is why the calculator rounds down.
