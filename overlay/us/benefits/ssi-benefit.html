How the SSI payment is calculated

Supplemental Security Income pays the federal benefit rate minus your countable income. Countable income is worked out in this order:

  1. Take unearned income (Social Security, pensions, unemployment, support from others) and subtract the $20 general exclusion.
  2. From earned income (wages, self-employment) subtract any unused part of the $20, then $65, then half of the rest.
  3. Add the two countable amounts and subtract them from $1,029 (individual) or $1,544 (couple).

Things that can lower or stop the payment

  • Living in someone else's household and not paying your share of shelter costs can cut the federal rate by one-third.
  • Free shelter from others can be counted as in-kind support (food is no longer counted since September 30, 2024).
  • Deeming: part of a spouse's or (for a child) parents' income and resources counts as yours.
  • Resources over the limit on the first day of the month mean no SSI for that month.
  • Living in a public institution or medical facility usually limits SSI to $30 a month.

Who can get SSI

  • People aged 65 or older, or blind, or disabled (adults and children).
  • Limited income and resources (see above).
  • U.S. citizens or certain qualified non-citizens, living in the U.S.

How to apply

Apply at ssa.gov/ssi, call 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778), or visit a local Social Security office. Report changes in income, resources or living arrangements by the 10th of the following month.

FAQ

How much is SSI in 2027?

The federal maximum is $1,029 a month for an eligible individual and $1,544 for an eligible couple, after the 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment announced on October 14, 2026. In 2026 it was $994 and $1,491. Many states add a supplement.

When is the first 2027 SSI payment?

SSI is normally paid on the 1st of the month. Because January 1, 2027 is a holiday, the January payment — the first at the new rate — is paid on December 31, 2026.

How much can I earn and still get SSI?

SSA ignores the first $20 of most income, the next $65 of earnings and half of the earnings above that. With no other income, an individual's federal SSI only reaches zero at about $2,143 a month of wages in 2027 ($1,029 × 2 + $85).

Does Social Security reduce my SSI?

Yes. Social Security retirement or disability benefits are unearned income, so after the $20 general exclusion they reduce SSI dollar for dollar. $400 of Social Security leaves about $649 of federal SSI in 2027.

What is the SSI resource limit?

$2,000 for an individual and $3,000 for a couple. Your home, one vehicle, household goods and some other items don't count.
