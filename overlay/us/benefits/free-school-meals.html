Free & reduced school meals

Enter household size and yearly income.

Check your numbers

Quick answer: for July 2026 – June 2027, a family of 4 qualifies for free school meals with income up to $42,900 a year ($3,575 a month) and for reduced-price meals up to $61,050 ($5,088 a month) in the 48 states and DC. Reduced-price lunch costs no more than 40¢.

How eligibility is worked out

USDA sets the limits each year from the HHS poverty guidelines: free meals = household income up to 130% of the poverty guideline, reduced-price meals = up to 185%, with the annual result rounded up to the next dollar. Monthly, twice-monthly, biweekly and weekly limits are the annual limit ÷ 12, 24, 26 or 52, also rounded up. The 2026 guideline for the 48 states and DC is $15,960 for one person plus $5,680 for each extra person.

What counts as income and household

  • Household: everyone living together who shares income and expenses, related or not — not just parents and children.
  • Income: gross income before taxes and deductions — wages, self-employment net income, child support, alimony, pensions, Social Security, unemployment benefits.
  • Pay frequency: if your income varies, the school converts it to a yearly amount; you can compare your paycheck directly with the monthly or weekly column above.
  • No application needed if your district directly certifies your children through SNAP, TANF or FDPIR — check with the school first.

How to apply

Fill in the free and reduced-price meal application from your child’s school district (online or paper) at the start of the school year or any time your income drops. One application usually covers all children in the household at schools in the same district.

FAQ

What is the income limit for free school lunch for a family of 4 in 2026–27?

$42,900 a year ($3,575 a month) in the 48 states and DC. Up to $61,050 a year ($5,088 a month) qualifies for reduced-price meals. These limits apply from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027.

What is the free lunch income limit for a family of 3?

$35,516 a year or $2,960 a month (130% of the 2026 poverty guideline of $27,320). Reduced price: up to $50,542 a year or $4,212 a month.

How much do reduced-price meals cost?

Schools can charge no more than 40 cents for a lunch and 30 cents for a breakfast. Many states and districts cover that amount, so reduced-price students may pay nothing.

Do we qualify automatically if we get SNAP?

Yes. Children in households receiving SNAP, TANF or FDPIR are categorically eligible for free meals, and most are directly certified by the school district without an application. Foster, homeless, migrant, runaway and Head Start children also qualify.

Can I apply in the middle of the school year?

Yes. Federal rules require schools to accept an application at any time during the school year, for example after a job loss. Last year's status carries over for up to 30 operating days of the new school year.

Why does my school give free meals to everyone?

Schools using the Community Eligibility Provision (CEP) serve free breakfast and lunch to all students without applications. A school, group of schools or district can choose CEP when at least 25% of students are identified through programs like SNAP.
