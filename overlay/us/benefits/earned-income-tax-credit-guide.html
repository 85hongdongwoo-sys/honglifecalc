Check your numbers

Quick answer: for tax year 2026 the EITC is worth up to $8,231 (3+ children), $7,316 (2), $4,427 (1) or $664 (none). A single filer with two children must earn under $58,629 ($65,899 married filing jointly). For 2025 returns filed in 2026 the maximum is $8,046.

How the EITC is worked out

Example first. A single parent with two children earns $25,000 in 2026.

  • Phase-in: the credit is 40% of what you earn until it reaches the maximum. With two children you hit the $7,316 maximum at $18,290.
  • Plateau: you keep the maximum until income reaches $23,890 (single) or $31,160 (married filing jointly).
  • Phase-out: above that, the credit falls by 21.06 cents for each extra dollar. $25,000 − $23,890 = $1,110, and $1,110 × 21.06% = $234. So the credit is $7,316 − $234 = $7,082.

The credit rates are 7.65% (no children), 34% (1), 40% (2) and 45% (3 or more). The phase-out rates are 7.65%, 15.98%, 21.06% and 21.06%. Refundable means you get the credit even if you owe no tax – it can come back to you as a refund.

Who can claim

  • You have earned income (wages or self-employment) under the limit.
  • You, your spouse and any qualifying child have a valid Social Security number issued by the return's due date.
  • Married people usually must file jointly. A separated spouse can claim in some cases.
  • A qualifying child must meet the age, relationship, residency and joint return tests.
  • Without a qualifying child, you must be at least 25 but under 65 at the end of the year.

How to claim and common mistakes

  • Claim on your return. File Form 1040 and add Schedule EIC if you have a qualifying child.
  • Free help: IRS Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA) sites and IRS Free File.
  • Don't miss it. The IRS estimates about one in five eligible taxpayers don't claim the EITC.
  • Only one person can claim a child. Agree with anyone else in the household who could claim the same child.
  • Expect a wait. Refunds with EITC can't be issued before mid-February.

EITC FAQ

How much is the Earned Income Tax Credit in 2026?

For tax year 2026 the most you can get is $8,231 with three or more children, $7,316 with two, $4,427 with one and $664 with no qualifying children. For tax year 2025 (returns filed in 2026) the maximums are $8,046, $7,152, $4,328 and $649.

What is the income limit for EITC?

For tax year 2026, single filers with two children must earn under $58,629, and married couples filing jointly under $65,899. With no children the limits are $19,540 and $26,820. Your investment income must also be $12,200 or less ($11,950 for 2025).

How much EITC will I get with $25,000 income and 2 kids?

For a single parent in tax year 2026, about $7,082. You are past the $23,890 point where the credit starts to fall, so the $7,316 maximum drops by about 21 cents for each extra dollar.

When will I get my refund if I claim EITC?

By law the IRS can't issue EITC or ACTC refunds before mid-February – and that holds up your whole refund, not just the credit. For the 2026 filing season the IRS said most early filers who chose direct deposit and had no problems would get their refund by March 2.

Can I get EITC without children?

Yes, if you are at least 25 but under 65 at the end of the year, have a valid Social Security number, and earn under $19,540 (single) or $26,820 (married filing jointly) in 2026. The most you can get is $664.

Can I claim EITC for past years?

Yes. The IRS says you have three years from your return's due date to claim a refund. If you filed but didn't claim the EITC, file an amended return on Form 1040-X.
