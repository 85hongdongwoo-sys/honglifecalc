2027 Federal Tax Brackets in Canada

Brackets, basic personal amount, CPP and EI for January 1, 2027 — with a 2026 vs 2027 calculator.

The CRA has published the 2027 federal tax brackets, indexed by 2.6%: 14% on the first $60,044, 20.5% up to $120,088, 26% up to $186,157, 29% up to $265,203 and 33% above. The basic personal amount rises to $16,879 (down to $15,214 for the highest incomes). They take effect on January 1, 2027.

Frequently asked questions

What are the 2027 federal tax brackets in Canada?

14% on taxable income up to $60,044; 20.5% from $60,044 to $120,088; 26% from $120,088 to $186,157; 29% from $186,157 to $265,203; 33% over $265,203 (CRA T4127, 124th edition, effective January 1, 2027).

What is the indexation rate for 2027?

2.6% (2026: 2.0%).

What is the basic personal amount for 2027?

$16,879 for net income up to $186,157, reducing gradually to $15,214 at $265,203 or more.

What are the 2027 CPP and EI maximums?

CPP: YMPE $76,700, YAMPE $87,400, maximum employee contribution $4,209.00 plus $428.00 CPP2. EI: maximum insurable earnings $70,800, employee rate 1.64%, maximum premium $1,161.12 (outside Quebec).

What is the federal minimum wage in 2027?

It is $18.15 an hour since April 1, 2026. The April 1, 2027 rate (indexed to inflation) has not been announced yet.
