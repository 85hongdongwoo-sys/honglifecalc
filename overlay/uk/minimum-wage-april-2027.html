Minimum Wage from April 2027: £13.21

All four minimum wage rates for 2027/28 and what your hours add up to.

From April 2027 the National Living Wage (21 and over) rises to £13.21 an hour, up 50p or 3.9% from £12.71. The 18–20 rate becomes £11.27, and the 16–17 and apprentice rates £8.31. The government accepted these Low Pay Commission recommendations on 28 October 2026. Income tax thresholds stay frozen: the personal allowance remains £12,570 until April 2031.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum wage in April 2027?

The National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over will be £13.21 an hour from April 2027.

How much is £13.21 an hour per year?

£13.21 × 37.5 hours × 52 weeks = £25,759.50 gross a year. At 40 hours it is £27,476.80.

What is the minimum wage for 18 to 20 year olds in 2027?

£11.27 an hour from April 2027 (up 42p).

Is the personal allowance going up in April 2027?

No. The personal allowance stays at £12,570 and the basic rate limit at £37,700 — both frozen until 5 April 2031.

Will public sector pay rise in 2027/28?

Pay review body awards for 2027/28 have not been announced yet. They usually come in spring or summer.
