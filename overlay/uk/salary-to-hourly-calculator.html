Your numbers

Quick answer: Hourly rate = salary ÷ (weekly hours × 52). £30,000 a year is £15.38 an hour at 37.5 hours a week. The National Living Wage is £12.71 from April 2026 and rises to £13.21 from April 2027.

How to convert a salary to an hourly rate

Hourly rate = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks paid per year). In the UK an annual salary normally pays for all 52 weeks, including your paid holiday, so you divide by 52 — not by the weeks you actually work. A 37.5-hour week therefore has 1,950 paid hours a year; a 40-hour week has 2,080.

To go the other way, salary = hourly rate × hours per week × 52. Weekly pay is salary ÷ 52 and monthly pay is salary ÷ 12.

Worked example

£32,000 a year, 37.5 hours a week: 37.5 × 52 = 1,950 hours. £32,000 ÷ 1,950 = £16.41 an hour. Weekly: £32,000 ÷ 52 = £615.38. Monthly: £32,000 ÷ 12 = £2,666.67. Daily (5-day week): £123.08.

Things that change the comparison

  • Paid holiday. Full-time workers get at least 5.6 weeks (28 days including bank holidays). An hourly or day-rate job without holiday pay is worth less than the same rate on a salary.
  • Unpaid breaks. If your contract is 40 hours on site with a 30-minute unpaid lunch, your paid hours are 37.5.
  • Overtime. Paid overtime isn't included in salary; add it separately.
  • Pension and benefits. Employer pension contributions (minimum 3% of qualifying earnings under auto-enrolment) and other benefits add to the value of a salaried job.

FAQ

What is £30,000 a year per hour?

£15.38 an hour on a 37.5-hour week (1,950 hours a year), or £14.42 on a 40-hour week.

What is £32,000 a year per hour?

£16.41 an hour at 37.5 hours a week, £15.38 at 40 hours. Weekly pay is £615.38 and monthly pay £2,666.67 before tax.

How many working hours are in a year in the UK?

A 37.5-hour week is 1,950 paid hours (37.5 × 52) and a 40-hour week is 2,080. Paid holiday is included in these figures because an annual salary covers holiday pay.

What is the minimum wage in 2026 and 2027?

From 1 April 2026 the National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over is £12.71 an hour and the 18–20 rate is £10.85. The government has announced £13.21 and £11.27 from April 2027.

What salary is the National Living Wage?

£12.71 × 1,950 hours = £24,784.50 a year on a 37.5-hour week, or £26,436.80 on a 40-hour week (2026/27 rate).
