Your numbers

Quick answer (2026/27): you can pay up to £20,000 a year into ISAs, and interest, dividends and gains inside are tax-free. Budget 2026 kept the cash ISA limit at £20,000 from April 2027 (the planned cut to £12,000 was reversed). £500 a month at 5% grows to about £77,641 in 10 years.

How much tax an ISA saves on interest

Outside an ISA, interest above your Personal Savings Allowance is taxed. Inside an ISA it is not. Example: £30,000 earning 4% a year makes £1,200 of interest.

How the calculator works

Each month the calculator adds growth to your balance, then adds your monthly payment. It repeats this for every month.

  • Monthly growth = balance × (yearly return ÷ 12).
  • Paid in = starting balance + monthly payment × number of months.
  • Growth = final value − paid in.
  • Allowance cap: payments over £1,666.67 a month (£20,000 a year) are cut back to that amount.

A cash ISA is like a savings account with tax-free interest. A stocks and shares ISA holds investments such as funds. They can earn more over long periods, but their value can also fall.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Use it or lose it. Unused allowance does not carry over to the next tax year.
  • Transfer, don't withdraw. To move an ISA to a new provider, ask the new provider to transfer it. Taking the money out yourself can lose its ISA status.
  • Check fees. On a stocks and shares ISA, a 1% yearly fee takes a big bite out of a 5% return.
  • Money protection. Cash in a cash ISA is covered by the FSCS up to £120,000 per person, per authorised firm.

ISA FAQ

How much can I put in an ISA in 2026/27?

£20,000 in total between 6 April 2026 and 5 April 2027. You can put it all in one ISA or split it, for example £15,000 in a cash ISA and £5,000 in a stocks and shares ISA. A Lifetime ISA can take up to £4,000 of it.

Is the cash ISA limit still going down to £12,000?

No. A cut to £12,000 for under-65s from April 2027 had been announced, but Budget 2026 on 28 October 2026 reversed it. HMRC says the £20,000 cash ISA limit will be kept from 6 April 2027.

How much will £500 a month in an ISA be worth?

After 10 years about £69,871 at 3% a year, or £77,641 at 5% a year. After 20 years at 5% it is about £205,517 – you paid in £120,000 and growth added about £85,517. Returns on shares are not guaranteed.

Do I need an ISA if I have the Personal Savings Allowance?

Maybe not for small amounts. A basic-rate taxpayer can earn £1,000 of interest a year tax-free anyway. But with £30,000 at 4%, a higher-rate taxpayer earns £1,200 of interest and pays £280 tax outside an ISA (£308 from 2027/28). Inside an ISA they pay nothing.

Can I pay more than £20,000 into ISAs?

No. £20,000 is the most you can pay into all your ISAs in one tax year, and it does not roll over. This calculator caps payments at £20,000 a year (£1,666.67 a month). If you think you have paid in too much, contact your ISA provider.

Is the Lifetime ISA worth it?

For a first home or retirement it can be: the government adds 25%, so £4,000 becomes £5,000. But taking money out for anything else costs 25% of what you take out, which is more than the bonus. For a first home over £450,000 the charge is 20%.
