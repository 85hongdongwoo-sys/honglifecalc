Your numbers

Quick answer (2026-27): a bonus is taxed as ordinary income at your marginal rate plus the 2% Medicare levy. On a $90,000 salary a $10,000 bonus costs about $3,200 in tax (32%), so you keep $6,800. Your employer can withhold at most 47% of a bonus; your tax return settles the difference.

How bonus tax works

Example first. You earn $90,000 and get a $10,000 bonus. Your income becomes $100,000. All of that bonus falls in the 30% bracket ($45,001 – $135,000). Add the 2% Medicare levy and the bonus is taxed at 32%: $3,200 tax, $6,800 to keep.

  • Marginal rate means the tax rate on your next dollar. It is the rate that matters for a bonus.
  • Bonus tax = tax on (salary + bonus) − tax on salary.
  • If the bonus crosses a bracket line, part is taxed at the lower rate and part at the higher one. Example: $120,000 + $20,000 = $140,000. $15,000 is taxed at 32% and $5,000 at 39%, so the tax is $6,750.
  • Withholding is the tax your employer takes out when it pays you. It's an estimate; your tax return works out the real figure.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Don't panic about the payslip. Withholding on a bonus can look high or low. What counts is your total income for the year.
  • Ask about salary sacrifice early. It must be agreed before you do the work.
  • Watch the Medicare levy surcharge. If a bonus lifts your income above $105,000 (singles, 2026-27) and you have no private hospital cover, a surcharge of 1% to 1.5% may apply.
  • Check your super. Make sure 12% super was paid on a qualifying bonus.
  • Remember HELP. A bonus can raise your study-loan repayment for the year.

Bonus tax FAQ

How much tax will I pay on a $10,000 bonus?

It depends on your salary. On $90,000 a year, a $10,000 bonus is taxed at 30% plus the 2% Medicare levy, so you pay about $3,200 and keep $6,800. On $60,000, a $5,000 bonus costs about $1,675 (34%), because the low income tax offset shrinks as your income rises.

Why does my bonus look so heavily taxed on my payslip?

Your employer works out withholding with the ATO's Schedule 5 methods, which spread the bonus across your pay periods. The amount held back can differ from your final tax, but it can't be more than 47% of the bonus. Your tax return settles the real amount.

Is a bonus taxed at a higher rate than my salary?

No. A bonus is ordinary income. It's added to your salary and taxed at the rates for your total income. Only the part of the bonus that pushes you into a higher bracket is taxed at that higher rate.

Do I get super on my bonus?

Usually yes. From 1 July 2026 super is paid on 'qualifying earnings'. The ATO lists performance, Christmas, sign-on and referral bonuses as qualifying. A bonus only for work done entirely outside ordinary hours is not. The super guarantee rate is 12%.

Can I salary sacrifice my bonus into super?

Yes, if you agree it with your employer before you do the work the bonus is for. Bonuses already earned can't be sacrificed. Salary-sacrificed amounts count towards the $32,500 concessional cap for 2026-27.

Does a bonus increase my HELP repayment?

It can. HELP repayments are based on your total repayment income, so a bonus can push you into a higher repayment band. In 2026-27 repayments start above $69,528. Choose 'Yes' for HELP debt in the calculator to see it.
