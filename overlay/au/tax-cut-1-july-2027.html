Tax Cut from 1 July 2027: What You Save

Compare your tax in 2026–27 and 2027–28.

From 1 July 2027 the tax rate on income between $18,201 and $45,000 drops from 15% to 14%. It was 16% until 30 June 2026. Anyone earning over $45,000 saves the full $268 a year compared with 2026–27 ($536 compared with 2025–26). The national minimum wage is $26.44 an hour ($1,004.90 a week) from 1 July 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What are the tax rates from 1 July 2027?

0% up to $18,200; 14% from $18,201 to $45,000; 30% from $45,001 to $135,000; 37% from $135,001 to $190,000; 45% above $190,000 (residents, excluding Medicare levy).

How much will I save from the 2027 tax cut?

Compared with 2026–27, 1% of your income between $18,201 and $45,000 — up to $268 a year. Compared with 2025–26 (16%), up to $536.

Is the 2027 tax cut law?

Yes. The ATO says the cuts to 15% (1 July 2026) and 14% (1 July 2027) are now law.

What is the minimum wage in Australia?

$26.44 an hour or $1,004.90 for a 38-hour week from 1 July 2026, after the Fair Work Commission Annual Wage Review 2026.
