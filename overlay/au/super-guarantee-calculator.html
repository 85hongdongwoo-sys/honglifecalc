Your numbers

Short answer: In 2026-27 your employer must pay 12% of your qualifying earnings into super, for example $10,200 a year ($392.31 a fortnight) on $85,000. Since 1 July 2026 (Payday Super) it must reach your fund within 7 business days of each payday. SG is only due on the first $270,830 of earnings, and it counts toward the $32,500 concessional cap.

Super guarantee figures for 2026-27

The super guarantee (SG) is the money your employer must pay into your super fund on top of your pay. These are the official ATO figures for 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

How the calculator works

Example first: $85,000 × 12% = $10,200 a year. Paid fortnightly, that is $10,200 ÷ 26 = $392.31 each payday.

  • Super a year = 12% × the smaller of your qualifying earnings and $270,830.
  • Super per pay = super a year ÷ number of paydays (52, 26 or 12).
  • Package: pay = package ÷ 1.12 (or package − $32,499.60 above the base).
  • After fund tax = super × 85%. Your fund takes 15% contributions tax.

Qualifying earnings is the new name (from 1 July 2026) for the pay super is worked out on. It is your ordinary time earnings – pay for your normal hours – plus commissions and amounts you salary sacrifice. Salary sacrifice means paying some of your before-tax salary into super.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Check your fund, not just your payslip. Since 1 July 2026 super should show up in your account within about 7 business days of each payday. Look in your fund's app or in myGov (ATO online services).
  • Package or plus super? A $90,000 job “plus super” is worth $10,800 more a year in total than a $90,000 package.
  • Salary sacrifice must not cut your SG. From 1 July 2026 salary-sacrificed amounts count in your qualifying earnings, so your employer still pays 12% on your full pre-sacrifice pay.
  • Watch the $32,500 cap. SG + salary sacrifice + deducted personal contributions over $32,500 are taxed extra.
  • Missing super? Ask your employer first. If it is still missing, the ATO has an unpaid super process.

Super guarantee FAQ

How much super is paid on $85,000?

12% of $85,000 = $10,200 a year. That is $196.15 a week, $392.31 a fortnight or $850 a month. After the 15% tax in the fund, $8,670 a year goes into your account.

What is the super guarantee rate for 2026-27?

12% of your qualifying earnings. It went up from 11.5% to 12% on 1 July 2025 and stays at 12% for 2026-27 and later years.

What is Payday Super?

From 1 July 2026 your employer must pay your super each time they pay your wages, not once a quarter. The money has to reach your super fund within 7 business days of payday (20 business days for a new employee's first payment).

What is the maximum super contribution base for 2026-27?

$270,830 a year. It is now a yearly limit, not $62,500 a quarter as in 2025-26. On $300,000 your employer only has to pay 12% of $270,830, which is $32,499.60, instead of $36,000.

My salary is $95,200 including super. How much is super?

$95,200 ÷ 1.12 = $85,000 pay and $10,200 super (12%). Choose “Including super (package)” in the calculator to split a package like this.

Does employer super count toward the concessional cap?

Yes. SG, salary sacrifice and personal contributions you claim a deduction for all count toward the $32,500 cap for 2026-27. On $85,000, SG uses $10,200, so about $22,300 is left for salary sacrifice.

Is super paid on overtime?

Usually not. Overtime is not part of qualifying earnings. Super is paid on ordinary hours pay, most allowances, commissions, shift loadings and salary-sacrificed amounts.
