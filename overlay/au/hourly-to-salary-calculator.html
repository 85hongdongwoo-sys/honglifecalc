Hourly to salary

Enter your hourly rate and hours. The standard full-time week in Australia is 38 hours.

Your numbers

Quick answer: annual salary = hourly rate × 38 hours × 52 weeks. $35 an hour is $69,160 a year ($2,660 a fortnight), about $56,509 after 2026-27 tax and Medicare. The national minimum wage from 1 July 2026 is $26.44 an hour ($1,004.90 a week).

How to convert an hourly rate to a salary

Annual salary = hourly rate × hours per week × paid weeks per year. Monthly = annual ÷ 12, fortnightly = annual ÷ 26, weekly = annual ÷ 52. A full-time year is 38 × 52 = 1,976 hours. Take-home pay uses 2026-27 resident tax rates, the low income tax offset and the Medicare levy. Super (12%) is paid by your employer on top unless your package is quoted “including super”.

Common mistakes

  • Using 40 hours instead of 38 — Australian full-time is usually 38 hours; check your award or agreement.
  • Comparing a casual rate with a permanent salary — the 25% casual loading replaces paid leave and other entitlements, so a casual hourly rate should be higher.
  • Forgetting whether super is included — “$80,000 including super” is a base salary of about $71,429.
  • Ignoring penalty rates and overtime — weekend, night and overtime rates under awards are paid at higher multiples; add those hours separately.
  • Fewer paid weeks — contractors without paid leave or public holidays should enter fewer weeks (for example 46–48).

FAQ

How much is $35 an hour per year in Australia?

$69,160 a year at 38 hours a week for 52 weeks ($2,660 a fortnight). After 2026-27 income tax, the low income tax offset and the 2% Medicare levy, take-home pay is about $56,509 a year or $2,173 a fortnight. Super of $8,299 (12%) is paid on top.

What is the minimum wage in Australia from 1 July 2026?

The national minimum wage is $26.44 an hour or $1,004.90 for a 38-hour week, up from $948.00 a week. Award- and agreement-free casual employees get at least a 25% casual loading on top. Many award rates are higher.

How many working hours are in a year in Australia?

A full-time week under the National Employment Standards is up to 38 hours, so a full year is 38 × 52 = 1,976 paid hours. Paid annual leave and public holidays are included in the 52 weeks.

Is $30 an hour a good wage?

$30 an hour full-time is $59,280 a year or $2,280 a fortnight before tax — about 13% above the national minimum wage of $26.44. Take-home is about $49,901 a year.

How do I convert a salary to an hourly rate?

Divide the annual salary by 1,976 (38 × 52). For example, $80,000 ÷ 1,976 = $40.49 an hour. If your package includes super, first divide by 1.12 to get the base salary.

What tax rates apply in 2026-27?

Resident rates: nil up to $18,200; 15% from $18,201 to $45,000 (down from 16%); 30% to $135,000; 37% to $190,000; 45% above. The Medicare levy of 2% is extra.
