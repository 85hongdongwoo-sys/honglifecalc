Your numbers

Quick answer: net worth = everything you own − everything you owe. $510,500 of assets (home, super, savings, car) minus $318,000 of debts (mortgage, loans, cards) = $192,500. Count super and your HELP debt, and use today's values.

How it works

Example first. You have $15,000 in the bank, $38,000 in super and shares, a home worth $450,000 and a car worth $7,500. That is $510,500 of assets. You owe $300,000 on the mortgage, $15,000 on a car loan and $3,000 on a credit card: $318,000 of debts. Net worth = $510,500 − $318,000 = $192,500.

  • Assets = things you own that have a money value.
  • Debts (liabilities) = money you owe.
  • Net worth = assets − debts.
  • Debt-to-asset ratio = debts ÷ assets. 62% means you owe 62 cents for every dollar you own.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Use today's values. Not what you paid for the house or car.
  • Don't forget small debts. Buy now pay later, phone plans with device repayments and tax you still owe all count.
  • Count your offset account as cash. Don't also take it off the mortgage, or you count it twice.
  • Track the same way each time. Then the change from year to year is meaningful.
  • Pay off the most expensive debt first. Credit cards usually cost the most interest, so clearing them grows your net worth fastest.

Net worth FAQ

How do I work out my net worth?

Add up everything you own, then take away everything you owe. Example: $510,500 of assets − $318,000 of debts = $192,500 net worth.

Should I include my super in my net worth?

Yes, because it is your money. But keep in mind you usually can't use it until you retire. That is why the calculator also shows your net worth without your home, so you can see how much you could actually reach.

Does my HELP debt count?

Yes, add it under other loans. It is money you will have to repay, even though it has no interest charge like a normal loan.

Should I count my car?

Yes, but at what it would sell for today. Cars lose value fast, so a car bought for $30,000 may only be worth $15,000 after a few years.

What is home equity?

Home equity is the part of your home you really own: its value minus the mortgage. A $450,000 home with a $300,000 mortgage gives $150,000 of equity.

Can my net worth be negative?

Yes. If you owe more than you own, your net worth is below zero. That is common when you are starting out or after study. Paying off high-interest debts first is usually the fastest way back above zero.

How often should I check my net worth?

Once or twice a year is enough for most people – for example every July after tax time. The trend matters more than any single number.
