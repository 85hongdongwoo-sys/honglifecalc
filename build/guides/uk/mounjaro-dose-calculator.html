Mounjaro in the UK · NHS, private & KwikPen

Mounjaro in the UK: who can get it on the NHS, what a KwikPen costs privately, and how the dose steps work

Short answer: in the UK, Mounjaro (tirzepatide) is licensed for weight management from a BMI of 30, or 27 with a weight-related condition. On the NHS in England it’s being rolled out in stages: in 2026/27, adults with a BMI of 35 or more and at least four qualifying conditions are the priority group (2.5 points lower for some ethnic backgrounds). Privately, the NHS list price is £133–£330 per KwikPen, and each pen lasts four weeks.

Since the four-dose KwikPen arrived in 2024, Mounjaro has become one of the most talked-about prescriptions in the UK – and one of the most confusing to navigate. Here’s a clear summary of how you can get it, what it costs and how the doses go up, based on the MHRA licence, NICE and NHS England guidance.

Getting Mounjaro on the NHS (England)

NICE approved tirzepatide for weight management in December 2024 (TA1026), but because so many people qualify, NHS England is rolling it out over up to 12 years, starting with the people who need it most. It can be prescribed by GPs as well as specialist weight services.

Everyone prescribed it on the NHS also gets wraparound support – diet, activity and behaviour advice for at least nine months. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland make their own decisions, so ask your GP practice what’s available locally.

Private prescriptions and prices

Many people use online or high-street pharmacies, which must carry out a clinical assessment (including your BMI and medical history) before prescribing. From September 2025, Lilly’s UK list prices rose sharply:

Only buy from a UK-registered pharmacy that asks for a consultation. The MHRA regularly seizes fake and unlicensed “weight-loss pens” sold through social media – these may contain the wrong dose or no tirzepatide at all.

Using the KwikPen

  • Each pen holds four weekly doses of one strength. You dial the dose and inject; there’s nothing to measure.
  • Start on 2.5 mg for 4 weeks, then usually 5 mg. Your prescriber may increase by 2.5 mg after at least 4 weeks on a dose, up to 15 mg.
  • Some liquid is left in the pen after the fourth dose. The licence says this extra solution must be thrown away with the pen – it isn’t a fifth dose, so don’t try to draw it out.
  • Keep unused pens in the fridge (2–8 °C, never frozen). After first use, a pen can be kept out of the fridge for up to 30 days at no more than 30 °C – then it must be thrown away.

Questions people ask about Mounjaro in the UK

Can my GP prescribe Mounjaro for weight loss?
Yes, if you fall into the current NHS priority group and your area’s service is up and running. GPs check your BMI, conditions and suitability, and you’ll be referred to wraparound support. If you don’t qualify yet, your GP can tell you when the next group is expected.
How much does Mounjaro cost per month privately?
Based on the list price of one pen every four weeks: about £133 on 2.5 mg, £180 on 5 mg, £255 on 7.5–10 mg and £330 on 12.5–15 mg. Online pharmacies’ prices vary, and some include the consultation.
Does Mounjaro stop the pill working?
It can make the combined or progestogen-only pill less reliable. The MHRA advises using a barrier method as well, or switching to a non-oral method, for 4 weeks after starting and for 4 weeks after each dose increase. It isn’t recommended in pregnancy, so talk to your prescriber if you’re planning one.
What if I miss my weekly injection?
If it’s been 4 days or less, inject as soon as you remember. If it’s more than 4 days, skip that dose and inject on your usual day. Never take two doses within 3 days of each other.
Can I take Mounjaro abroad on holiday?
Yes – keep it in your hand luggage with your prescription or a letter from your prescriber, and check the rules of the country you’re visiting. After first use, a pen can stay out of the fridge for up to 30 days at no more than 30 °C.
