Ovulation & fertile days · a gentle guide

When am I most fertile? Ovulation day by cycle length, the signs to look for, and what the calendar can’t tell you

Short answer: ovulation usually happens about 14 days before your next period, not 14 days after the last one. With a 28-day cycle that’s around day 15, and your most fertile days are roughly days 10 to 16. The two or three days just before ovulation give the best chance of pregnancy.

If you’re trying for a baby, the waiting can feel long, and the advice online can be confusing. The good news is that the basic timing is simple once you know one thing: the part of the cycle after ovulation is fairly steady at around two weeks, while the part before it is what varies. That’s why the calculator works backwards from when your next period is due.

Signs your body gives you

  • Cervical mucus turns clear, slippery and stretchy, a bit like raw egg white, in the most fertile days.
  • Ovulation (LH) test strips turn positive roughly 24–36 hours before you ovulate – a helpful confirmation of the calendar estimate.
  • Basal body temperature rises slightly (about 0.2–0.5 °C) after ovulation. It confirms that ovulation happened, so it’s more useful for learning your pattern over a few months than for timing this month.
  • Some people notice a one-sided twinge in the lower tummy, breast tenderness or a change in libido.

Questions people ask about ovulation

How many days after my period do I ovulate?
It depends on your cycle length rather than your period. With a 28-day cycle and a 5-day period, ovulation is usually about 10 days after your period ends. With a 32-day cycle it’s later; with a 24-day cycle it can be only a few days after your period finishes.
Can I get pregnant just before or after my period?
It’s less likely, but possible – especially with short cycles. If you ovulate early (say around day 8), sperm from sex at the end of your period can still be alive when the egg is released.
What if my cycles are irregular?
Use your shortest recent cycle to find the earliest possible fertile day and your longest to find the latest, and use ovulation test strips to narrow it down. If your cycles are often shorter than 21 or longer than 35 days, or very unpredictable, it’s worth mentioning to your doctor.
How often should we have sex when trying to conceive?
Every one to two days during the fertile window works well. There’s no need to time it to the hour – regular sex across the window covers the days that matter.
How long does it usually take to get pregnant?
About eight in ten couples under 40 conceive within a year of regular unprotected sex. If you’ve been trying for a year (or six months if you’re 35 or over), see a doctor – earlier if you have irregular periods or a known health issue.
