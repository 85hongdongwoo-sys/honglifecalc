Have I hit Coast FIRE? How much you need saved today at each age, and what “coasting” really looks like

Short answer: you’ve reached Coast FIRE when your current savings, left alone to grow, will reach your full FIRE number by retirement. With a $1 million target and a 5% real return, you need about $231,000 invested 30 years before retirement, about $377,000 with 20 years to go, or $481,000 with 15 years to go.

Full early retirement asks you to save 25 times your spending. Coast FIRE is a gentler milestone: once you reach it, you can stop saving for retirement altogether and only earn enough to pay today’s bills. Many people use it to switch to part-time work, a lower-stress job or a career change without falling behind.

Why starting early matters so much

Look at the table: the amount you need now more than doubles between 35 and 15 years before retirement. Money invested in your twenties has decades to compound, so a modest balance early can do the work of a much larger one later. That’s why Coast FIRE is often reached in a person’s thirties even if full FIRE is far off.

Questions people ask about Coast FIRE

What return should I assume?
Use a real (after-inflation) return. Many planners use 4–6% for a diversified stock-heavy portfolio. A lower number is safer: at 4% instead of 5%, the 30-year coast number for $1 million rises from about $231,000 to $308,000.
Do I stop investing completely once I hit Coast FIRE?
You can stop adding to retirement savings, but you still need to cover living costs and ideally keep an emergency fund. Many people keep contributing a little anyway, which builds a margin for bad markets.
What’s the difference between Coast FIRE and Barista FIRE?
With Coast FIRE your investments aren’t touched until retirement – work pays all current bills. With Barista FIRE you’ve already started drawing on investments and use part-time income to cover the gap.
Does Coast FIRE include pensions?
It can. If a workplace pension or Social Security will cover part of your spending, your FIRE target – and therefore your coast number – is smaller.
