Centimetres, inches, feet, metres and miles

cm to inches, feet to metres, miles to km – the conversions people look up most, with height and running charts

The key numbers: 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly, 1 foot = 30.48 cm, 1 metre = 3.281 feet, and 1 mile = 1.609 km. So 5′10″ is 177.8 cm, a 10K race is 6.2 miles, and a 65-inch TV is 165 cm across the diagonal.

Length is where metric and imperial cross paths most often – heights on forms, screen sizes, furniture measurements, running distances, road signs on holiday. The converter above does any unit; these tables cover the everyday ones.

Mental shortcuts

  • Miles to km: multiply by 1.6 (or add 60%). 50 miles ≈ 80 km.
  • Km to miles: multiply by 0.6 – or use the Fibonacci trick: 5 km ≈ 3 mi, 8 km ≈ 5 mi, 13 km ≈ 8 mi.
  • Inches to cm: multiply by 2.5 for a quick estimate, then add a little.
  • Metres to feet: multiply by 3 and add 10%. 2 m ≈ 6.6 ft (exact: 6.56).

Questions people ask about length conversion

How many cm is 5 feet 7 inches?
170.2 cm. Work it out as (5 × 12 + 7) × 2.54 = 67 × 2.54.
How many inches is 30 cm?
11.81 inches – just under a foot, which is 30.48 cm.
How are TV and phone screen sizes measured?
Diagonally, corner to corner, in inches. A 55-inch TV is 139.7 cm across the diagonal; for a 16:9 screen it’s about 121.8 cm wide and 68.5 cm tall.
Is a nautical mile the same as a mile?
No. A nautical mile is 1,852 metres (about 1.15 ordinary miles) and is used at sea and in aviation.
How many feet is 1 metre?
3.281 feet, or 3 feet 3.37 inches.
