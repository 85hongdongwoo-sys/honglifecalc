Kilograms, pounds, stones and ounces

kg to lbs (and back) – quick tables, stones for UK readers, and easy tricks to convert in your head

The exact numbers: 1 kg = 2.20462 lb and 1 lb = 0.453592 kg. In your head: double the kilograms and add 10% – 70 kg × 2 = 140, plus 14 = 154 lb. Going the other way, halve the pounds and take off 10%: 180 lb → 90 → 81 kg (exact: 81.6 kg).

Weight conversions come up constantly: a recipe from another country, luggage limits, a gym programme in pounds, or a doctor’s scale in kilograms. The converter above handles any unit; the tables below cover the numbers people look up most.

Questions people ask about weight conversion

How many pounds is 1 kg?
2.20462 pounds. For everyday use, 2.2 is close enough – the error is less than 0.1%.
How do I convert stones and pounds to kg?
Turn everything into pounds first, then multiply by 0.4536. For 11 st 6 lb: 11 × 14 + 6 = 160 lb, and 160 × 0.4536 = 72.6 kg.
How many grams are in an ounce?
28.35 grams in one (avoirdupois) ounce, and 16 ounces in a pound. 100 g is about 3.5 oz. Gold and silver use troy ounces, which are heavier at 31.1 g.
Is a US ton the same as a metric tonne?
No. A US (short) ton is 2,000 lb or 907 kg; a metric tonne is 1,000 kg (2,205 lb); a UK (long) ton is 2,240 lb or 1,016 kg.
Why do airlines say 23 kg or 50 lb?
They’re treated as the same limit, but 50 lb is actually 22.7 kg – slightly less than 23 kg. If you’re close to the limit, go by the unit the airline’s scale shows.
